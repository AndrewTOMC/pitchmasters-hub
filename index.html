    Make three minutes count

    Three minutes, then the bell. No slides, no lectern, just you and the room. Here's what wins it, and space to draft your own.

    Storytelling

    Open on a moment, not a mission statement. One person, one turning point, one thing that changed. A room forgets your stats by the bar, but it remembers a story all the way home. Facts tell, stories sell.

    Presenting

    Plant your feet and pick three faces in the crowd to talk to, like friends. Slow down. A held pause pulls a room in closer than any clever line. Let your hands stay still until they have something to say.

    Business talk

    Say the problem so plainly a child would nod along. Then your fix, one proof that it already works, and a clear ask. Bring the one number that matters, not a spreadsheet. Clarity is the whole game.
